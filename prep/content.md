## Part A: existing technology

##### Software
* The first app I looked up what **Medisafe**
  * Medisafe is an app that sends reminders to users to take medications.
  * It sends the user notification on upcoming medical appointments.
  * This app is normal used when people have a lot of mediacations they need to take at once.
  * [Medisafe offical website](https://medisafe.com)
* The second app i looked at was **Bearable**
  * This app is used to track your symptoms.
  * It's mostly used when people have a hard time remembering something when speaking to a doctor.
  * Bearable doesn't only track sleep as it tracks many other things such as sleep, mood and energy.
  * Bearable also sends reminders to log any symptoms that someone might experience.
  * Bearable also has a free and paid version of there app (Free vs Premium).
  * [Bearable offical website](https://bearable.app)
* The third app I looked at was **MyChart**
  * This app was created by the Epic (The Epic that allows you to read books) for NYC health hospitals and was made so doctors can contact patients.
  * It also allows patients to request prescription refills and review medical records
  * MyChart also allows you to schedule appointments
  * Another feature is that you can check your results and pay for your medical bills from the app
  * [Offical NYC health hospital website](https://www.nychealthandhospitals.org) 


##### Hardware
* Nebulizer
  * A nebulizer converts liquid in a mist that is breathed through a mask, this is incase someone can't use a normal inhaler.
  * The average cost of a nebulizer is $40-60.
  * You can find one at your local pharmacy or you can order one online. 
  * [Picture of a nebulizer](https://cdn-gx-ai-media.oai.operacdn.com/public/generated_images/c83e99cf-dc27-11f1-97d9-0f3902a8b3fc/nebulizer_medical_device_5af555f2.jpg).
* Negative Pressure Wound Therapy Pump
  * A negative pressure wound pump helps wounds heal faster by removing fluid and bacteria.
  * The average cost is around $200-800 depending on the brand.
  * Not every wound should be treated with this device.
  * [Information on it](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/17313-negative-pressure-wound-therapy).
* Ultraviolet disinfection device
  * It uses UV-C lights to disinfect rooms.
  * The average one cost $1000-1500 due to it needing safety features.
  * The UV-C lights that i produces can harm your skin and eyes.
* Intraosseous access drill
  * A Intraosseous access drill is used when a doctor or anyone doesn't have access to an IV and is in a critical situation.
  * It cost around $1000 and can only be bought from professional medical suppliers.
  * Only professionals should use it as if you use it wrong it could lead to an infection, bone injury or tissue damage.
  * The access is only temporary and the drill should not be used multiple times in one day.
