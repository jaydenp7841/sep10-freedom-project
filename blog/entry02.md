# Entry 2: Medical Practice  
##### 11/23/26

## Content

Since the last blog I have found a couple of medical devices that are currently available for everyone to purchase. The device I want to look into the most is the **Negative Pressure wound Therapy Pump**, this device i able to heal certain wounds a lot faster by removing fluid and bacteria, which was backed up by this [website](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/17313-negative-pressure-wound-therapy). There are also apps that help people keep track of their own health, apps like _Mychart_ which is the official NYC health hospitals app that allows you to schedule appointments, request prescription refills, and review medical records. This app and the medical devices are pieces of technology that are impacting the current state of the medical field. There are other devices that help people such as the nebulizer which converts liquid into mist, which helps anyone that can't use a normal inhaler. Many more technologies are currently being created but are not getting the attention that they deserve as not many people are aware that technology could greatly benefit the medical field.

## Engineering Design Process (EDP)

I am currently on the second step of the design process, this step is all about researching the problem. The problem that I'm hoping to get more information on is why **Advances in medical devices aren't being prioritized enough**. Many of the medical devices I listed above are great examples of what technology can do to benefit the medical field. Many people overlook the fact that improving our medical technology could have a massive affect on how people get treatment in the future. I will probably be on this step for a while as the problem is not very talked about.

## Skills

One of the skills that improved was **How to Google**, as finding trusted information is difficult. Most information online isn't always trustable, by remembering that _.gov_ and _.edu_ we're always trustable, I was able to find information that I knew was supported by many.

Another skill that I improved was **How to read**. This skill allowed me to find sections within articles that aligned with what I was researching about. I also made sure to read everything in the article as some lines with in article can provide the missing piece I need to advance to the next step in the design process.

One skill that I learned is Problem decomposition, this skill allowed me to split up the work needed for researching into different parts. In this way I wouldn't get burnt out from trying to research everything in one sitting.
