##### 10/26/26

## Why Medical Practice?

The topic I have choose for the year-long project is **Medical Practice**, I choose this topic because I have always had a passion for the Medical field. I have always been interested in this field since I learned about it and to incorporate mew technology for the benefit of others is something I could see myself doing in the future. Most people have been to the doctor if not everyone, and all the technology they used has been refined to give results and treatment plans, and that's where I see the beauty in Medical Practice as some people have put so many hours just to help others. The other topics I had in mind were business, which I choose because I wanted to look into how people can make a fortune off of a basic idea and how technology has made it easier. The other topic I was thinking about was airlines/aerospace, I was interested in this topic because of how pilots and anyone else involved in flying a plane can have so much teamwork and precision so make sure the result is exactly the same every time. The 2 links that had me thinking about these topics were [Research: Technology Is Changing How Companies Do Business](https://business.cornell.edu/news/2024/05/16/research-technology-is-changing-how-companies-do-business/) by SC Johnson College of business, and [Exploring Emerging Technologies for Advanced Aerospace Efficiency](https://www.intechopen.com/chapters/1194024) by Abhay Dhasmana and Sachin Srivastava, these 2 articles led me to researching more about them but the article that made me research Medical Practice more was [7 Essential Technologies for Medical Practice Management](https://digitalscientists.com/blog/essential-technologies-medical-practice-management/) by Bob Klein. Seeing how the use of technology can impact how people are treated is fascinating and it made me even more interested at what else technology could be used for in the medical industry.

## Engineering Design Process

I am currently in the first stage of the Engineering design process, where the main idea is to define a problem. The problem I hope to solve with my project is that **Medical devices aren't being used enough**. For example, there could be so many more medical devices that could improve the health of so many people but no one has thought of trying to create it, and if anyone decided to create it, it could help so many people. 

## Skills

When I searched for my topic _"new technologies in the medical field"_ it showed me a ton of results, and if I were to look through all of them I would have been there for the rest of my life. By looking at the title and the small description I was able to find articles that fit what I was talking about. 

Another skill that goes with how to google is **How to read** as an article can have a lot of information that is useful, but not useful for the topic I'm researching about, so by reading sub headings I was able to find information faster. This is also the reason that not just me but everyone is adding sub headings to there blog, so in that way the reader can have an easier time finding information. 

The last skill I learned was **Time Management** as I have been getting good amount of work from all my other classes, so I needed to manage my time so that way I was able to make the best possible assignment/project to submit to each of them. I also tend to procrastinate so I have been putting more time into my day just to do assignments
